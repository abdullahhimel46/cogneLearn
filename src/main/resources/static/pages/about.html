About Us

Built for deep work. Reclaim your focus, unlock your learning potential.

What is cogneLearn?

cogneLearn is an AI-powered adaptive learning platform designed to merge the vast educational repository of YouTube with distraction-free tracking tools and real-time attention insights.

Powered by a Spring Boot backend and secure session-based authentication, cogneLearn gives learners a structured, personalized, and privacy-conscious environment for deep, focused study.

The Problem We Are Solving

YouTube is arguably the most powerful university ever created, housing millions of high-quality tutorials, bootcamps, and lectures. However, it is also one of the most hostile environments for deep learning:

  • The Recommendation Loop: Recommendations and visual clutter draw you away from your curriculum and into mindless entertainment loops.
  • Passive Consumption: It is easy to watch hours of educational videos without actually absorbing or retaining the core insights.
  • The Privacy Paradox: Existing telemetry tools constantly monitor, sell, and build invasive advertising profiles on your intellectual interests.

Our Solution

cogneLearn acts as a protective shield for your focus. We provide a beautifully crafted sandbox that filters out all the noise of YouTube while supercharging the learning process with active feedback systems:

  • Distraction-Free Sandbox: Only your curated videos are visible. No comments, no related recommendations, no sidebar popups.
  • AI Attention Tracking: An optional local AI tracker evaluates your real-time engagement and provides constructive focus feedback — all face-detection processing happens in your browser.
  • Secure Cloud Persistence: Your session history, streaks, and playlists are stored securely on the server with BCrypt-encrypted credentials. No third-party tracking, no ad profiles.

Core Features

  • Sleek Playlist Manager: Easily build and customize custom learning paths from any YouTube URL.
  • Unified Pomodoro: Structured work-to-break intervals to protect your cognitive stamina.
  • Attention Heatmaps: Track your focus levels and study frequencies over days and weeks.

Our Philosophy

At cogneLearn, we live by a singular, guiding belief: "Built for Deep Work."

True learning is not passive—it is an active, focused dialogue between the mind and the material. By giving you the environment and tools to measure and master your focus, we hope to make self-directed education rewarding, robust, and completely free of tracking anxiety.

Built By

cogneLearn is developed with care by passionate learners who were tired of getting distracted while studying complex engineering subjects online. Built with a Java 21 + Spring Boot 3 backend, session-based Spring Security, H2 persistence via JPA/Hibernate, and a modern vanilla HTML/CSS/JS frontend — all engineered to deliver a premium, blazing-fast learning experience for students around the world.
