Privacy Policy

Privacy by design. Your data belongs to you, and stays with you.

Introduction

cogneLearn is designed and built with a privacy-first philosophy. We believe that your study habits, learning progress, and personal focus data are deeply personal. Our platform is engineered from the ground up to respect your confidentiality and ensure that you retain full ownership of your learning metrics.

Data Collection

Unlike traditional SaaS platforms that silently harvest user analytics and learning behaviors to build tracking profiles, cogneLearn implements strict constraints on data access:

  • No Personal Tracking: We do not gather or log your learning behaviors, clicked videos, or attention durations on any external servers.
  • Minimal Server Interaction: Our backend APIs only manage basic authentication sessions and essential study structures. The detailed telemetry of your study sessions is processed completely locally.

Local Storage & IndexedDB

To provide a robust, persistent experience without relying on server-side tracking, cogneLearn makes extensive use of secure web databases inside your own browser:

  • Your focus metrics, study history, Pomodoro sessions, and custom playlists are saved locally in your browser's localStorage and secure database instances.
  • This information never leaves your device and is never synchronized with third-party tracking clouds.

Webcam Usage & AI Monitoring

One of cogneLearn's core features is intelligent attention tracking to analyze your focus levels. We handle this feature with extreme care:

  • Real-time processing only: The attention tracking engine processes your webcam frames locally, in real-time, completely within your browser using client-side libraries.
  • Zero Storage: We do not record, save, or store your video feeds, images, or raw facial details under any circumstances.
  • Zero Transmission: Your camera feed never leaves your local computer. It is never transmitted across the network, ensuring complete privacy during study sessions.

Third-Party Services

To access video materials, cogneLearn embeds YouTube videos using official player integrations:

  • When playing videos, you interact directly with YouTube's embedded system.
  • YouTube's own privacy guidelines and tracking policies apply to the video content being viewed. We recommend checking YouTube's privacy policy for details on how they manage your viewing interactions.

User Control

You have full, absolute control over your environment at all times:

  • You can turn off the webcam attention monitoring feature at any time with a single click.
  • You can completely wipe all of your stored focus statistics, playlist history, and local analytical data from your browser settings or by clearing your browser cache.
