User Guidelines

Your ultimate handbook to unlocking hyper-focus and deep learning with cogneLearn.

1. Getting Started

Ready to supercharge your learning journey? Getting started with cogneLearn is incredibly easy:

  • Create an Account: Register to establish your private profile, where your basic study metadata is securely initialized.
  • Import Playlists: Click the "+ Add Playlist" button on your dashboard. You can paste any direct YouTube video URL or a complete YouTube playlist URL. cogneLearn will parse and index them instantly into a clean, distraction-free environment.

2. The Focus Session Flow

Our focus player is optimized to help you enter deep states of cognitive flow:

  • Select a Topic: Open your study playlist and select the lesson or video you wish to learn.
  • Launch the Pomodoro: Align your session with structured focus cycles (typically 25 minutes of work followed by a 5-minute rejuvenating break).
  • Single-tasking: Minimize external browser tabs, silence notifications on your phone, and focus entirely on the video and study window.

3. AI Attention Tracking & Setup

cogneLearn uses intelligent real-time face tracking to determine if you are actively engaged with your studies:

  • Camera Alignment: Place your laptop or webcam at eye level. Ensure your face is centered within the camera view frame.
  • Adequate Lighting: Maintain consistent, soft lighting in your room so the local AI libraries can accurately track facial indicators. Avoid strong backlights (like open windows directly behind you).
  • Calibration: Give the platform a few seconds when launching a session to establish your baseline focus posture.

4. Best Practices for Deep Work

To get the absolute most out of cogneLearn, we recommend these daily habits:

  • Study in Blocks: Rather than studying for 6 unstructured hours, execute three highly focused 90-minute blocks with deliberate breaks.
  • Leverage the Heatmap: Review your daily Consistency Heatmap on the dashboard. Maintaining a daily streak—even for just 20 minutes a day—builds robust, lifelong neural habits.
  • Review Analytical Insights: Regularly study your focus-to-distraction ratio to understand when your energy levels peak during the day.
