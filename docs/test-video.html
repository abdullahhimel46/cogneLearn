How to find a YouTube video ID:
  • Open any YouTube video
  • Look at the URL: youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID_HERE
  • The VIDEO_ID is always 11 characters
  • Example: jNQXAC9IVRw (YouTube's intro video)
